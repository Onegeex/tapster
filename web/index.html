Prices any realm can trust, and read for free.

Reporters stake GNOT and post prices every round. The answer is the stake-weighted median. Reporters close to it earn from the reward pool; reporters far from it lose part of their stake, which refills the pool. Honest reporters get paid by dishonest ones.
